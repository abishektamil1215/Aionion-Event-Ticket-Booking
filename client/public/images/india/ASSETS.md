# Indian professional imagery

Original AI-generated demonstration imagery created with the built-in imagegen tool for this project. Portraits depict fictional Indian professionals; they are not photographs of real Aionion employees or speakers. Conference, workshop and networking scenes are illustrative rather than documentation of actual events.

## Files and prompt set

Shared direction: one photorealistic professional image per asset, realistic skin texture, restrained navy/ivory/warm-neutral palette, no text, logos, watermark or public-figure resemblance.

- `conference.webp`: wide premium Chennai investor conference, Indian audience and female finance speaker, believable modern hotel auditorium, blue and ivory stage lighting.

Portraits retain their full composition. WebP versions are encoded for the website, and small `*-avatar.webp` variants keep attendee avatars lightweight. Generation originals remain under the imagegen output directory. The site uses local assets, so these faces do not depend on a third-party image host.
